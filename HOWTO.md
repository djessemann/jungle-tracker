# How to use DUBPLATE

This is the same text the app shows on the **?** page, at the bottom right.
If you change one, change the other — it lives in `HELP` near the end of
`index.html`.

## Play

Press the triangle. There is already a tune in there. Press it again to stop.

## The grid

Four rows. BREAK is the drums, BASS is the low end, STAB is the chords, FX is
sirens and crashes.

Sixteen squares across is one bar of music.

## Making a beat

Tap a pad at the bottom. You hear it, and it is now the sound in your hand.

Tap squares in that row to put it there.

Tap a square again to take it out. Hold a square to wipe whatever is in it.

## Rows

Tap a row name to work on that row. The pads change with it.

Hold a row name to mute the row. Hold it again to bring it back.

## REV and ROLL

Turn REV on and the squares you tap play backwards.

Turn ROLL on and they stutter three times, like a snare roll.

They stay on until you turn them off, so you can paint a run of them.

## A B C D

Four versions of the same sixteen steps.

Tap one to go there. If it is playing, your tap waits for the bar to finish,
and the one you asked for goes orange until it lands.

CHN plays every version that has something in it, round and round.

## Your own break

Go to BREAK and press LOAD. Pick any drum loop off your phone.

It gets trimmed, timed and cut into slices for you.

Press FILL BEAT and the slices land on the grid in the right order, at your
tempo.

Now move them around. That is the whole game.

## Cutting it up

8, 16 and 32 cut the loop into even pieces. AUTO cuts on the hits instead, and
SENS is how fussy AUTO is.

Drag any line on the picture to move a cut. Tap a slice to hear it.

BARS is how long the loop is. If it sounds half speed or double speed, change
it.

USE BPM sets the tune to the speed of the break.

## Sound

VOL, TONE and FX belong to the row you are on. FX is delay and reverb.

The number at the top is the tempo. Drag it up or down.

SWING on the SAVE page loosens the timing.

## Keeping it

It saves itself as you go. SAVE gives it a name and keeps it in the list.

WAV makes an audio file of the loop.

SEND makes one file with your break inside it, so you can move it somewhere
else.

## What is in it

The eight built in breaks are not recordings. They are the rhythms played on a
drum machine that lives in the app, so nobody's record is in here.

Your imports are yours.
